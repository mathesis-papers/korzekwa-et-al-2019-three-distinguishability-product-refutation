# Counterexamples to Korzekwa et al. (2019), Section III.B, p. 6, Eq. (30): extending constructions of Bengtsson et al. (2005) and Korzekwa et al. (2019)

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

For the product-structure conjecture of Korzekwa, Czachórski, Puchała and Życzkowski (2019), Section III.B, Eq. (30) of arXiv:1812.09083v3, we extend constructions of Bengtsson et al. (2005) and Korzekwa et al. (2019, Appendix C.1) by identifying a two-parameter family of counterexamples and giving a further counterexample outside that family. All are common diagonals of three orthogonal pure states and fail the proposed form under every vertex ordering. A rational example is (1/6,1/4,7/24,7/24); its total-variation distance from the union of proposed product forms is at least 1/192. The obstruction is an exact rank-one test on the residual barycentric weights.
